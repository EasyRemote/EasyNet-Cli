# Invariants

- Every product-complete capture target must bind a canonical selected Resource URA and a session id.
- Display, window, and application capture must preserve the intended capture scope.
- Window/application capture must not silently fall back to display capture.
- Rendered frame evidence must bind the selected Resource, session, frame source, geometry revision, and selected sentinel.
- Terminal receipts must remain visible and bound to the session.
- Product-completion aggregation validates summaries only; deep raw evidence remains owned by the cross-platform capture verifier.
